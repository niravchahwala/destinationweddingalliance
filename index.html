What We Offer

Built for Meaningful Partnerships.

DWA is not a directory. Not a marketplace. It is a curated, members-only alliance — where relationships are built, trust is earned, and bookings follow naturally.



View Membership Tiers
01

FAM Trips — Purposeful, Not Touristic

Curated 3–4 day immersions for 10–15 qualified Indian wedding planners at your property. They experience your destination as their clients would — and return as your most powerful advocates.

02

Community Visibility Inside WedIQ

Your destination featured across the WedIQ ecosystem — WedIQ Wire newsletter, WedGPT.ai platform, World Wedding Week, and social communities of 1,000+ curated wedding professionals.

03

World Wedding Week Stage

WWW 2027 — April 5–11 — our annual global hybrid event. DWA destination partners are featured as preferred partners in front of a worldwide wedding professional audience.

04

Long-Term Relationship Building

Annual FAM trips, recurring features, and ecosystem integration that builds lasting booking relationships — not one-time campaigns that disappear.
